Nook 0.8.3 — Quick Share-style cross-device sync.

- Scan the receiver's QR code to connect instantly — no more typing addresses.
- Desktop "Send via QR": Windows/Linux shows its QR, your phone scans it, and
  the PC pushes the selected notes back.
- Android Wi-Fi Direct discovery: find devices on different Wi-Fi networks.
- mDNS discovery reliability fixes and a crash fix when stopping discovery.

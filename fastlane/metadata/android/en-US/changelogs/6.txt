Nook 0.8.4 — sync reliability fixes.

- Doodles are no longer lost when syncing — strokes survive the transfer.
- Pairing now needs both devices to accept the same PIN before anything moves,
  and a failed connection offers a retry instead of silently proceeding.
- mDNS discovery fixed so nearby devices actually show up on the Send screen.
- Sharper, higher-contrast QR codes and a prominent "Scan a sender's QR code"
  action on the receive screen.

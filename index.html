Nook is a private, local-first note-taking app built with Flutter. No account. No cloud. No telemetry. Your notes are encrypted on your device, and when you want them on another one, they travel directly over your Wi-Fi — never through a server.

"Every big note app makes you trade data ownership for good design. Nook refuses that trade."

Google Keep and Apple Notes are lovely to use — and cloud-tied, closed, and built around an account you don't control. Nook works fully offline, forever. There's no sign-in screen and no "continue in browser."

Sync is a feature you opt into, not the price of entry: your notes beam directly from one of your devices to another over nearby Wi-Fi, with nothing in between. And because Nook is GPL-3.0, anyone can read it, change it, or build on it — nobody can quietly take it closed later.

Three promises

The motto splits into three clauses — each one a commitment, not a slogan.

Your notes
Full-text search, checklists, doodles, per-note theming. Nook helps you make more of your notes — it never locks them away.
Your device
Encrypted SQLite storage, keys held in the platform keystore, a biometric gate on the app itself. Nothing leaves unless you say so.
Yours
GPL-3.0, source and all — every line readable, every path open to fork, extend, or reuse.

What's inside

Everything a serious notes app needs, without asking for your data in return.

Notes

Create, edit, pin, lock, color and cover-image every note.

Organization

Notebooks, tags, multi-tag filtering, and full-text search (FTS5).

Checklists

Toggle items, drag to reorder, and track progress at a glance.

Doodles

Freehand drawing with smooth, pressure-aware strokes.

Editor

Block-based rich editing — text, checklists and doodles in one note.

Theming

Material You 3 dynamic color, per-note overrides, light and dark.

Security

SQLCipher encryption at rest, biometric gate, screenshot blocking.

Device-to-device sync

Peer-to-peer over Wi-Fi — discovery, pairing and transfer, no server involved.

Export

Take everything with you as a portable .nook bundle, any time.

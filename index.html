01 Your notes

Full-text search, checklists, doodles, per-note theming. Nook helps you make more of your notes — it never locks them away.

02 Your device

Encrypted SQLite storage, keys in the platform keystore, biometric gate. Nothing leaves your device unless you say so.

Notes

Organization

✅

Checklists

Toggle items, drag to reorder, and track progress at a glance.

Doodles

Pressure-aware freehand drawing with smooth, geometric shape recognition.

✏️

Block Editor

Rich block-based editing — text, checklists and doodles in one note.

🎭

Theming

Material You 3 dynamic color, per-note overrides, light and dark modes.

🔒

Security

SQLCipher encryption at rest, biometric vault, screenshot blocking.

Nook is in alpha.
